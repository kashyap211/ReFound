About the project

ReFound is an IoT-based smart lost-and-found system. A Raspberry Pi 3 Model B+ with a camera, IR sensor, push button, buzzer, LEDs and an OLED display detects items. It sends them to this Flask backend, which stores them in SQLite and shows them on this dashboard.

The Raspberry Pi connects through POST /api/detection and POST /api/system-status (see README.md).
